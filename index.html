About

I am a Master's student in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge at the University of Bologna, with a background in English Literature. My work focuses on the digital representation of historical and archival materials through RDF/RiC-O, SPARQL, TEI/XML, IIIF, and digital research interfaces.

Projects

These projects reflect my development as a Digital Humanities researcher, with recurring interests in semantic modeling, cultural heritage data, digital scholarly editions, knowledge organization, and research-oriented interfaces.

ResearchSpace Interface for G. Albini Archive

Current thesis-related project · RDF/RiC-O · SPARQL · ResearchSpace · Archival interface

A Digital Humanities project focused on representing and navigating archival hierarchy through RDF/RiC-O data and ResearchSpace. The work includes semantic tree navigation, SPARQL query design, search logic, path-to-root behaviour, document preview, and image integration.

My contribution: SPARQL query testing, interface logic, archival hierarchy analysis, document preview integration, and workflow documentation.

View project fund ·

Academic context: Developed as part of the final thesis project work under the supervision of prof.Francesca Tomasi, University of Bologna.

Susan B. Anthony Diaries — Digital Scholarly Edition Prototype

Individual project · TEI/XML · IIIF · Manuscript transcription · Digital edition

A prototype digital scholarly edition of selected Susan B. Anthony diaries, focused on TEI/XML modeling, manuscript image alignment, diary-page structure, and annotation planning.

My contribution: TEI/XML structure, diary-entry modeling, image-text alignment logic, and edition design.

View project · View repository ·

Academic context: Developed as part of the course Digital Scholarly Editing: Theory, Methods and Practice (1) (LM) in the Master's programme in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge, University of Bologna.

FairTOlogy — Fairytale Ontology Project

Group project · Ontology design · Perspectivisation · Narrative modeling

An ontology project modeling fairy tales and adaptations across cultural and historical contexts. The model includes character archetypes, moral themes, ending types, literary modes, and a perspectivisation frame for comparing traditional tales with modern retellings.

My contribution: Ontology alignment, entity specification, taxonomy design, and conceptual documentation, narrative analysis, and RDF generation.

View repository

Academic context: Developed as part of the course Knowledge Representation and Extraction (1) (LM) in the Master's programme in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge, University of Bologna.
